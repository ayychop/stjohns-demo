Privacy Policy

This is the website of St. John's Mini Market, 90 St. Johns Ave, London NW10 4EG. We are a corner shop, not a data company, and this site is built to collect as little about you as possible.

What we collect

Nothing, directly. The site has no contact forms, no accounts, no newsletter sign-up and no analytics or advertising trackers. We do not know who visits.

If you phone us, we will see your number and whatever you tell us, and we use that only to answer you.

Cookies

The site itself does not set any cookies. There is one thing on it that can:

  • The Google map. The map on the home page is provided by Google, and Google sets its own cookies when it loads. We only load it if you choose "Accept" on the cookie banner or press "Show the map". If you say no, the map stays switched off and nothing is sent to Google.

Your choice is remembered in your own browser (in local storage, not a cookie) so we do not have to keep asking. You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the home page.

Other companies

Links to Google Maps take you to that service, which has its own privacy policy. Our web hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and time of visit) to keep the site running and secure.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Ask in the shop or call 020 8965 9565. If you are unhappy with how we have handled something, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change how the site works, we will update this page and the date at the top.
